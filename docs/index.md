# D2 Loot Alert

A free, unofficial fan app for **Destiny 2** that tells you when the loot you want is
easy to get:

- **Bonus focus drops:** a notification on the day a weapon you watch is an activity's
  bonus focus.
- **Featured raids and dungeons:** a notification in the week a raid or dungeon you
  watch is featured.
- **Xûr:** a notification when Xûr is selling an exotic armor piece you watch.

## How to use it

1. Browse **TODAY**, **LOOT** or **RAIDS & DUNGEONS**, and tap the **bell** next to
   anything you want. The first time, the app asks to send notifications; allow it.
2. That's it. When something you watch is available, you get a notification, and it
   shows up at the top of **TODAY** under **READY TO FARM**.

**When alerts arrive:**

- Bonus focus alerts come shortly after the **daily reset** (17:00 UTC).
- Raid and dungeon alerts come after the **weekly reset** (Tuesday, 17:00 UTC).
- Xûr alerts come when he arrives (Friday, 17:00 UTC).

## Troubleshooting

**No notifications?**

- Check that notifications are allowed for D2 Loot Alert in your phone's settings.
- Check that the bell is filled in (gold) for the items you want.
- Alerts only come when something you watch is actually available that day or week.

**Images missing?** The app tries again by itself once your connection is back. If
they're still missing, close the app and open it again.
